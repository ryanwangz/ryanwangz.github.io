Hello! 

I'm a bioengineering PhD student and [Knight-Hennessy Scholar](https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/people/ryan-wang) at Stanford University, advised by Frank Willett and Jaimie Henderson in the [Neural Prosthetics Translational Lab (NPTL)](https://nptl.stanford.edu/). I am also supported by the [National Defense Science and Engineering Graduate Fellowship (NDSEG)](https://ndseg.org/) and [McCoy Ethics Fellowship](https://ethicsinsociety.stanford.edu/about/graduate-student-fellows). 
<!-- previously had (https://profiles.stanford.edu/wang-ryan) link -->

My research interests lie at the intersection of neuroscience and engineering. I am currently developing presurgical mapping and neural decoding methods for communication restoration in aphasic stroke populations via brain-computer interfaces. I previously developed methods for noninvasive modulation and monitoring of the brain with [Dr. Jerzy Szablowski](https://www.szablowskilab.org/) at the [Rice Neuroengineering Initiative](https://neuroengineering.rice.edu/) and developed neural decoders for brain-computer interfaces with [Dr. Amy Orsborn](http://faculty.washington.edu/aorsborn/index.html) at the University of Washington's [Center for Neurotechnology](https://centerforneurotech.uw.edu/). I also studied sleep with Dr. Lauren Eggert and vaccination with Drs. Malathi Srinivasan and Shanthi Kappagoda at the [Stanford Center for Asian Health Research and Education](https://med.stanford.edu/care.html).
